What is in here, and how it got here

Every role on this dashboard has been opened and read against the live posting before it was published. Nothing is listed on the strength of a search-result summary. Four things are checked on each one:

  • Still open - no expired, filled or closed postings
  • Genuinely 100% remote, and remote for someone living in Maryland. If a posting never actually uses the word remote, it is dropped rather than assumed
  • Senior Director or VP - a role titled Director, or one whose body text reads Director, does not belong here
  • The salary band that applies to Maryland, not a higher band published for another state

Roles that fail any of these are removed entirely rather than kept at a lower score. That is why the list is short. A shorter honest list is the point.

Reading a role card

The number in the ring is an overall fit score out of 100. Green is a strong fit (85+), amber is good with a gap (75-84), orange is a stretch (65-74).

Under the salary you may see an amber warning that the band starts below $225K. That means the employer published a wide range and the top of it is not guaranteed - worth knowing before you invest time.

The grey line at the bottom shows how old the posting is, when it closes, and the date it was last checked against the live listing. Anything closing within seven days is highlighted in amber.

Click Details on any card to open three things: a five-part score breakdown explaining exactly why it scored what it did, what is known about the company, and a free-text notes box that saves as you type.

Tracking roles you are interested in

Every card has a Save to pipeline menu - choose Interested, Applied, Screening, Interview or Offer to track the role. Choose Save to pipeline again to untrack it.

Tracked roles appear on the Pipeline tab as a board, one column per stage, and the arrows under each card move it forward or back. Your notes travel with the role.

Your tracking survives the daily refresh. Each role is remembered by company and title rather than by position in the list, so a role you marked Applied stays Applied tomorrow even though the list around it changes.

Sending roles to yourself

The buttons at the top of the Pipeline tab work on whatever you have tracked:

  • Email to me - opens Gmail with a message already written - add your own address - listing every tracked role grouped by stage with links, salary and your own notes. You just press send.
  • Copy list - puts the same text on your clipboard to paste into a document, a message, or an email to someone else
  • Download CSV - a spreadsheet of everything you are tracking, for Excel or Google Sheets
  • Backup and Restore - see the caveat below

Your tracking lives in this browser only. It is not stored on a server. If you clear your browsing data, or switch to a different computer or phone, it will not follow you.

Use Backup now and then to download a small file of your pipeline, and Restore to load it back or move it to another device.

New jobs and finding your place

Jobs added since your last visit carry a New badge, and the line above the cards says how many there are. Choose Sort: new since last visit to put them first. Once you scroll past the filters, a search bar stays pinned to the top of the screen; Filters takes you back to the full set.

Gap analysis and tailored resume

Every card has two links, Gap analysis and Tailor resume:

  • Gap analysis - compares the posting with your resume: what you match, what's missing, which gaps are hard barriers and which you can close or explain, and whether it's worth applying.
  • Tailor resume - rewrites your resume for that posting, using only what's already in it. Anything the job asks for that you don't have is listed separately instead of being added.

Each link prepares a prompt with that job's details. Click Copy prompt, then open whichever AI assistant you use - ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot or another - start a new chat, attach your resume, paste and send. Your resume and the results stay in your own chat - nothing is posted to this page. Always read a tailored resume before you send it.

What the tiles at the top mean

  • Verified roles - the total, all individually checked and all 100% remote
  • High fit - scored 85 or above
  • Min clears floor - roles where even the bottom of the published band clears your floor for that track ($225K Executive, $175K PM / RMF). This deliberately does not count roles that merely could reach a high number
  • In pipeline - how many you are tracking

Roles closing within 7 days are counted on the line under the tiles, and flagged on their cards.

The Analytics tab

Six views of the same data: where the remote executive market actually is by sector, how scores are distributed, published salary bands with a dashed line marking your $225K floor, an average of where this slate is strong and where it is thin, your application funnel, and which kinds of executive roles exist remotely.

The compensation chart is worth a look - the grey part of each bar is the band minimum and the blue part is the range above it, so you can see at a glance which employers start below your floor.

What this dashboard does not know

Company intel is incomplete. Recent news and hiring history are unknown for most employers here, and those fields say so rather than guessing.

Where an employer publishes no salary, the card says Not disclosed - est. and the number is an estimate, not something the employer stated.

Most importantly: at Senior Director and VP level, a large share of roles are never posted publicly at all - they are filled through executive search firms and referrals. Treat this as one input to your search, not the whole map of it.

When it updates

The list refreshes every morning and you get an email summarising what changed. The date under the header always shows when roles were last checked against their live postings.
